# 20/20 Vision on Plant Extirpation

![orchid search](/projects/images/Hannah_Harvey_orchid_search_3.JPG#floatright)

This project aimed to establish a novel protocol for the detection of species extirpation, drawing on historical baseline data and contemporary citizen science observations recorded on the Biodiversity Galiano project using iNaturalist. After five years of extensive community-based search efforts, we have identified 14 vascular plant species that are at risk of extirpation from Galiano Island. Through targeted search efforts covering historical collection sites and suitable habitat, this project tested the potential for citizen science to detect ecological changes and inform conservation strategies with reference to historical baseline information.

This protocol is now published in [*Plants, People, Planet*](https://doi.org/10.1002/ppp3.70130), with a step-by-step walk-through of the analysis in our [protocol documentation](https://imerss.github.io/detecting-local-extinction/docs/).

Elements of this protocol are now being integrated into the [Salish Sea biodiversity atlas](https://salishseabiodiversity.org/) we are developing to support the assessment of biodiversity change across the region.
