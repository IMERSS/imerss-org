# Diatoms of the Salish Sea

![diatom SEM](images/SHW-Stub-13-TM4000.jpg#floatright) 

Diatoms are a major group of photosynthetic micro-algae living in marine and freshwater environments, which provide vast quantities of oxygen, food and nutrients for a wide range of organisms. The last regional diatom inventories were undertaken by Cupp (1943) for the West Coast of North America and Shim (1976) for the Georgia Strait, to the exclusion of many benthic and epiphytic species. This project aims to establish an updated taxonomic inventory of diatoms known to the Salish Sea, including detailed descriptions, discussions, observations and references. We apply light microscopy and Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM) to determine species based on key taxonomic features. To extend this work, we are also exploring the application of next-generation sequencing to barcode diatoms and promote community access to genomics research. In 2026, we published an [annotated checklist of 924 diatom taxa known to the Salish Sea](https://doi.org/10.3897/BDJ.14.e189060) in the open access *Biodiversity Data Journal*, including 42 species newly reported for the region.
